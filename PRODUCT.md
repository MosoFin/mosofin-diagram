# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fractional CFOs, bookkeepers, controllers, founders, and the AI agents that assist them. They need to explain how money moves through a small business — across Shopify, Stripe, a bank, and QuickBooks or Xero — to an owner, a board, or an auditor, and they need the explanation to be exactly as true as the brief that produced it.

## Product Purpose

Mosofin turns a finance brief plus one plain-language question into a checked, interactive money map, order path, revenue walk, close runbook, dispute lifecycle, payout reconciliation, A/R map, or cash-runway view. Success means a non-finance reader understands the primary story in under a minute, a finance reader can inspect every hop and its source of truth, and nobody is misled by a number, a "connected" arrow, or a green tick that the user never supplied. Scoped Route / Reach Share Card variants carry one path or one closure into a client note without pretending to be the whole map.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, accountable. Mosofin should feel like a good controller's whiteboard: clear about what is a fact, what is an assumption, and what is unknown; visually composed enough to put in front of an owner; never decorative at the expense of a tie-out.

## Anti-references

- Dashboards that imply live ledger data when they are drawings.
- Reconciliation views that show green because two systems are "connected", not because a difference is zero.
- Generic Mermaid beautifiers that change themes without improving the money story.
- Motion-first graph demos that imply flows or activity not present in the brief.
- Dense card grids, decorative glass, gradient text, and other AI-generated interface clichés.

## Design Principles

1. Truth before spectacle: every node, arrow, amount, and receipt derives from the brief or an explicit user statement.
2. One source of truth per fact: orders, cash, and books each name exactly one system of record.
3. One money story first: make the primary path legible before secondary flows and detail.
4. Progressive disclosure over permanent chrome: keep the canvas dominant; supporting detail lives in cards.
5. Motion has one bounded owner: static meaning must remain complete, motion must be finite and reader-controlled, and exports stay clean.
6. Portable proof: the default result is a self-contained artifact with deterministic validation and no required hosted runtime; Route and Reach Share Card variants name their scope.
7. Identity without semantic drift: an optional built-in or digest-pinned brand mark may identify Shopify, Stripe, or QuickBooks, but never replaces the node type, label, or relationship contract.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, dark and light themes, non-color state cues, readable contrast, and `prefers-reduced-motion` behavior. Desktop is the primary surface; narrow screens receive safe containment and usable controls. Viewer state, animation, and interaction must never be required to understand canonical exports.

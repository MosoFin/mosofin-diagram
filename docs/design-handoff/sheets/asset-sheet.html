Brand marks assets/favicon · specimen-glyphs

The favicon is the clearest inherited-identity problem. A cyan hexagon on #083344, used unchanged on all four site pages — nothing about it is finance. Three drawn replacements beside it, each shown at 56 / 28 / 16 px (the sizes a favicon actually lives at): the footed total (check over the accountant’s double underline — the recommended one), the tie-out (the proposed decision sigil promoted to brand mark), and the orange plate (loudest in a tab tray).

Brand-mark plates assets/brand-marks · 2 shipped + 16 added

Vendor colour is contained inside a neutral 16×16 white plate with a #cbd5e1 frame, so it never competes with the semantic node vocabulary — the rule is kept for every added mark. Shopify and Stripe are the two the delivered artifacts ship; the rest were pulled from the MIT-licensed ComposioHQ/open-logos library behind composio.dev/toolkits and closes most of the finance catalogue gap. QuickBooks and Bill.com exist there only as raster art — flagged for a vector redraw. Still missing from any source here: Chase / a generic bank, Mercury, Plaid, Brex, Adyen.
